Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

Do you take custom orders?

Yes! We love creating custom cakes, pastries, and bread for special events. Please contact us at least 48 hours in advance for custom orders.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes, we offer local delivery within a 10-mile radius. Delivery charges apply based on distance. You can select delivery at checkout.

Do you have gluten-free options?

Absolutely! We have a dedicated gluten-free section in our bakery. Check our products page and filter by dietary requirements.

Can I place an order for pickup?

Yes! You can place your order online and select "Pickup" at checkout. We'll notify you when your order is ready.
