The Beginning in Pune

Artisan Bakery was founded in 1995 by the Sharma family in the heart of Koregaon Park, Pune. What started as a small home-based bakery with traditional Indian and continental recipes has grown into one of Pune's most beloved bakeries.

Mr. Rajesh Sharma and Mrs. Priya Sharma, with their passion for baking and commitment to quality, built Artisan Bakery from the ground up. Using recipes passed down through generations combined with modern baking techniques, they created the perfect balance of tradition and innovation.

Today, we're proud to serve thousands of happy customers across Pune, employing local talent and sourcing ingredients from Maharashtra's finest farms. We've become more than just a bakery – we're a part of Pune's food culture.
